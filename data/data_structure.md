The arrest data used in this project comes from Jacob Kaplan's Concatenated files for the 2019-2024 NIBRS. Two NIBRS segments are required to replicate our analysis:



* Property: https://www.openicpsr.org/openicpsr/project/118281/version/V11/view?path=/openicpsr/118281/fcr:versions/V11/property\_segment\_parquet\_1991\_2024.zip\&type=file
* Arrestee: https://www.openicpsr.org/openicpsr/project/118281/version/V11/view?path=/openicpsr/118281/fcr:versions/V11/arrestee\_segment\_parquet\_1991\_2024.zip\&type=file



Download both of these files, unzip them, and put the contents in this folder.
